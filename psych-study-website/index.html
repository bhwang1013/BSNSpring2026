Ch 10 — Sexuality

Gender Dysphoria

  • NOT identity itself — it's the distress from mismatch between assigned sex & gender
  • Trap: Transgender ≠ disorder. Dysphoria = distress/impairment.

Sexual Dysfunctions — 3 Buckets

Ch 9 — Eating & Sleep

Eating Disorders — Easy Points

Anorexia

  • Starving + distorted body image
  • Severe weight loss
  • Restricting or purging type

Bulimia

  • Binge → purge cycle
  • Weight may be normal
  • CBT = strongest tx

BED

  • Binge ONLY — no purge
  • Marked distress
  • SSRIs reduce urges

That's literally 90% of exam questions on this topic.

Sleep Disorders

Insomnia

  • Can't fall/stay asleep
  • Performance anxiety about sleep
  • CBT-I is first-line

Narcolepsy

  • Sudden sleep attacks
  • Cataplexy (muscle collapse)
  • Trap: Cataplexy NOT always present

Ch 11 — Schizophrenia

Core Features

  • Disorganized thinking
  • Hallucinations (false perception)
  • Delusions (false belief)
  • Emotional dysfunction

Trap: Delusion ≠ Hallucination — know the distinction cold.

Phases — Very Testable

1 Prodromal

Weird behavior, social withdrawal, declining function

→
2 Active

Full psychosis — hallucinations, delusions

→
3 Residual

Lingering negative symptoms, reduced severity

Treatment

  • Antipsychotics (biological)
  • Psychosocial therapy
  • Combo > either alone

  • Schizoid
  • Schizotypal

Cluster B — Dramatic/Emotional

  • Antisocial
  • Borderline
  • Histrionic
  • Narcissistic

Cluster C — Anxious/Fearful

  • Avoidant
  • Dependent
  • OCPD

Diagnosis Issues (Essay Fuel)

  • Significant symptom overlap between disorders
  • Subjectivity in clinical judgment
  • Cultural and gender bias in diagnosis

A-Tier Essay Template

Use this exact flow for any disorder essay question:

  1. Define the disorder "[Disorder] is characterized by…"
  2. Key symptoms List 3–4 core symptoms, positive/negative if applicable
  3. Causes — 3 perspectives Biological · Psychological · Sociocultural
  4. Treatment Meds → therapy → combo approach and why combo wins
  5. Real-world implication Impact on daily function, relationships, nursing relevance

MCQ Strategy

  1. Identify the category firstWhat chapter/cluster/bucket does this fit?
  2. Match symptoms to disorderRule out distractors by checking defining feature
  3. Watch for trapsTransgender ≠ disorder · Cataplexy not always present · Insanity defense is rare
