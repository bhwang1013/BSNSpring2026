Getting Started

How AI Workflows Work

Each workflow is a repeatable system: choose the right tool, use the right prompting style, label your chats consistently, and save the outputs that matter.

🎯

Right Tool for the Job

Different AI models excel at different things. Match the task to the tool — speed, depth, creativity, or fun.

🏷️

Label Everything

Use the format (Date) (Role) (Topic) for every chat so you can find it again later.

💾

Save What Matters

Move useful outputs to Google Drive with labeled folders per AI tool. Public links let you share work instantly.

🔁

Casual Prompting

You don't need perfect prompts for most tasks. Talk to AI like a smart friend — be direct and conversational.

⬆️

Prompt Stacking

For complex work, use one AI to generate an optimized prompt, then feed it to a more powerful model.

🧘

Balance Screen Time

Build in regular Digital Detox breaks to reset mentally, spiritually, and physically.

🌿 Digital Detox

Reset & Recharge

AI tools are powerful, but your mind, body, and spirit need regular breaks. Don't stop until you notice a genuine positive shift.

🧘
Physical Reset
Static stretching routine
1
Begin Static Stretching
2
Do not stop until you have a noticeable positive shift in physical state
Static stretching (holding positions 20–60s) reduces tension and signals your nervous system to calm down.
🙏
Spiritual Reset
Repeating mantra
1
Choose a mantra of max 8 words
2
Repeat it continuously
3
Do not stop until you have a noticeable positive shift in spiritual state
Keep the mantra short and meaningful. Examples: "I am calm, focused, and present." or "One step at a time."
🌬️
Mental Reset
Progressive breathing protocol
1
2s in / 2s out — ~1 minute
2
3s in / 3s out — ~1 minute
3
5s in — ~1 minute
4
5s out — ~1 minute
5
6s in / 6s out — ~1 minute
6
Do Nothing — ~1 minute
7
Do not stop until you have a noticeable positive shift in mental state
This is box/resonance breathing. The 6s rhythm (~5 breaths/min) activates the parasympathetic nervous system for deep calm.
The goal isn't a timer — it's a felt shift. Each protocol ends when you genuinely feel better, not just when the clock runs out. Pair all three for a full reset.
